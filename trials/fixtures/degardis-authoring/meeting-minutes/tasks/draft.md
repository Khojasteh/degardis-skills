---
title: Write meeting minutes
cues:
- the requester supplies a meeting transcript or notes and asks for minutes
- the requester asks for problems in existing minutes to be fixed
goal: Minutes that let someone who was not in the meeting learn what was discussed and what was decided.
knowledge:
- source-material
- decisions-and-discussion
- minutes-structure
handoffs:
- task: review
  applicability:
  - When the requester asked for problems in existing minutes to be fixed without naming them, and no review on this route has identified them
---

Work only from the supplied source, and ask the requester for it when none was
supplied. When fixing problems in existing minutes, whether the requester named
them or a review found them, change only the parts they concern and keep the
rest as written.

1. Read the whole source before writing, and mark each passage as discussion
   or decision.
2. Write the minutes in the structure the minutes guidance gives.
3. Before delivering, check that every decision in the minutes traces to a
   passage in the source, and remove any that does not.
