---
title: End-user applications
category: Product
description: Releases people install and use directly, such as desktop, mobile, and web apps.
---

Readers of application release notes are users, most of whom read only the
first line or two, often on an app store's update screen or an in-app "What's
new" panel. They recognize features by the names on screens and menus, not by
internal component names.

App stores cap the length of update text, and the cap differs by store. Where
the notes will appear in a store listing, the requester or that store's own
publishing guidance supplies the limit.
