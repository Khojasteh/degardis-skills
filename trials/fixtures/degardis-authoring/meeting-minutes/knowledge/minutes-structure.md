kind: guidance
title: Minutes structure
---

Write minutes in three sections, in this order:

1. **Summary:** two or three sentences on the meeting's purpose and outcome,
   for a reader who reads nothing else.
2. **Discussion:** one short paragraph per topic, in the order discussed,
   attributing a position to a speaker only where the source names them.
3. **Decisions:** each decision on its own line, worded as the meeting settled
   it.

Keep the minutes short enough to read in a few minutes, leaving out side
conversations and repetition.
