# Pricing

## Volume discount

Every order earns a volume discount based on its subtotal, before tax and
before shipping.

| Order subtotal | Discount |
| --- | --- |
| Under $100.00 | none |
| $100.00 or more | 2% |
| $500.00 or more | 5% |
| $1,000.00 or more | 10% |

Only one tier applies to an order: the highest one the subtotal reaches. A
subtotal that lands exactly on a threshold earns that threshold's tier.

Discounts are applied to the subtotal and rounded to the nearest cent, with
halves rounded up.

## Worked example

A cart of four $125.00 items has a subtotal of $500.00, earns the 5% tier, and
is invoiced at $475.00.

## Taxes and shipping

Tax and shipping are calculated on the discounted subtotal. Neither is part of
the subtotal that selects the tier.
