---
title: Migration section
applicability:
- When the release contains a breaking change
- When readers must change their code, configuration, or data to upgrade
---

Add a Migration section directly after Breaking changes. For each breaking
change, give the reader's action as a numbered step: what to change, from what
to what, and how to tell it worked. If the change list does not say what a
reader must do, ask the requester rather than inventing a step.
