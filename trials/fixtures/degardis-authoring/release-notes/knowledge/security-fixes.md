---
kind: guidance
title: Security fixes
---

An entry for a security fix tells readers what is affected, how serious it is,
and what to do, usually which version to upgrade to. It leaves out how the flaw
can be exploited, because the notes reach attackers as well as the readers who
have not upgraded yet. Cite an advisory or vulnerability identifier when the
change list gives one. Anything further, such as crediting the reporter or
describing the flaw in detail, follows the requester's disclosure policy; when
the notes need it and no policy was given, ask the requester.
