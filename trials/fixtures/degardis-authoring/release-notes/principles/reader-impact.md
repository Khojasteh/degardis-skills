---
title: Reader impact
---

Release notes fail when a reader who must act on a change never notices it: a
breaking change or security fix buried under routine improvements is read too
late or not at all, and the reader learns of it from a failed upgrade. Give
every change prominence in proportion to what it demands of the reader and how
soon, never in proportion to how much work it was for its authors. When two
placements seem equally good, choose the one that still serves a reader who
stops reading early.
