---
kind: guidance
title: Section order
---

Order sections by what they demand of the reader and how soon, and leave out
any section that would be empty:

1. Breaking changes: the reader must act now to keep working.
2. Security fixes: the reader should upgrade soon to stay safe.
3. Deprecations: the reader must act before a later release.
4. New features
5. Improvements
6. Bug fixes

A reader who stops after the first screen has then still seen everything that
asks something of them.
