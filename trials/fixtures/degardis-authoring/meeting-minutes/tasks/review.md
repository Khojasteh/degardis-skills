---
title: Review meeting minutes
cues:
- the requester asks for an assessment of existing minutes
goal: A list of the problems in the supplied minutes, each tied to the passage of the source it misrepresents or omits, with the minutes themselves left unchanged.
knowledge:
- source-material
- decisions-and-discussion
- minutes-structure
handoffs:
- task: draft
  applicability:
  - When the requester asked for the problems found to be fixed and no correction of the minutes on this route covers them
---

Compare the minutes with the supplied source, and ask the requester for it when
none was supplied: without it, a review can judge only the minutes' form.

Report each problem with the passage it concerns: a decision the minutes record
that the source does not support, a decision in the source the minutes leave
out, discussion presented as a decision, or a departure from the minutes
structure.
